# TallyLoop: competitor page (fictional)

TallyLoop is a fictional company used in a course exercise. Nothing here describes a real product.

## What it sells

TallyLoop is a lightweight tracker for open items that come out of meetings: who owns what, and by when. It sends a short digest every Monday.

## Pricing

- Free: up to 5 people and 50 open items.
- Plus: 8 dollars per user per month, unlimited items and email reminders.
- Org: 15 dollars per user per month, adds reports and an export.

## Strengths

Very low price, simple to learn, reminders that people actually read.

## Weaknesses

It does not read meeting notes, so someone has to type each item in. There is no link back to the message or document where the item was first raised. Teams over 30 people say the digest becomes too long to be useful.

## Who it targets

Small teams and volunteer groups that already hold regular meetings.
