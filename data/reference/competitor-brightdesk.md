# BrightDesk: competitor page (fictional)

BrightDesk is a fictional company used in a course exercise. Nothing here describes a real product.

## What it sells

BrightDesk is a shared inbox and help desk for small teams of two to fifteen people. It routes email and chat to the right person and tracks replies.

## Pricing

- Starter: 19 dollars per user per month, one shared inbox, email only.
- Team: 39 dollars per user per month, adds chat, saved replies and a weekly report.
- Business: 79 dollars per user per month, adds single sign-on and an audit log.

There is a 14-day free trial. Annual billing takes 15 percent off.

## Strengths

Fast setup, a clean interface, good saved replies. Customers praise the weekly report.

## Weaknesses

No automatic summaries of long threads. Search finds exact words only, so a customer who writes "my payment did not go through" is not matched to a past ticket about a "failed charge". Reviewers also mention that the Team plan has no phone support.

## Who it targets

Small online shops, local service businesses and early-stage software teams.
