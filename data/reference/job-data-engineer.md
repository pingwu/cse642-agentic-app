# Job description: Junior Data Engineer, Lakeview Systems (fictional)

This posting is fictional sample text for a course exercise. Lakeview Systems does not exist, and the role is not open.

## About the team

Lakeview Systems builds scheduling software for community clinics. The data team of four keeps the reporting database accurate and fast. We work from a hybrid office, three days a week.

## What you will do

- Maintain nightly ETL jobs written in Python and SQL, and fix them when they fail.
- Add checks that stop a bad load before it reaches a report.
- Move two legacy spreadsheet reports into the warehouse.
- Write short notes on how each pipeline works so a teammate can run it.

## What we look for

- One to two years of Python and SQL, or a strong portfolio project.
- Comfort with Postgres, Docker and Git.
- A habit of testing small pieces before connecting them.
- Clear writing.

## Nice to have

Experience with a workflow scheduler, a cloud warehouse, or a language model used for data cleanup.

## Pay and growth

Salary range 78,000 to 96,000 dollars, plus a learning budget. Engineers who show good judgment on data quality move to a mid-level role in about 18 months.
