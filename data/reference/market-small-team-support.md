# Market note: support tools for very small teams (fictional)

This note is fictional sample data written for a course exercise. The figures are invented.

## Size and growth

In this fictional market, about 340,000 teams of two to fifteen people use a shared inbox or help desk. The count grew roughly 11 percent last year. Most new teams start on a free or starter plan.

## What buyers say they want

1. Setup in under an hour, with no consultant.
2. A price that does not rise sharply when the team adds one person.
3. Search that finds past answers even when the customer uses different words.
4. A weekly summary the owner can read in five minutes.

## Where buyers are unhappy

Existing tools search by exact words. Owners report that they answer the same question many times because they cannot find the earlier reply. Price jumps between the starter and team plans are the second most common complaint.

## Opportunity

A tool that finds past answers by meaning, summarises each week, and keeps a flat price up to fifteen users has a clear gap to fill. A fast pilot with ten shops would show whether owners pay for it.
